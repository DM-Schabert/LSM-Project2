Efficiency falls with P only where a step is short

Efficiency E = S / P, with the speed-up S = T(serial) / T(P) of the step loop. Work per step grows like N², the cost that does not shrink with more ranks hardly depends on N.

How the numbers were made, and what they leave out
  • amdahl/run_all.sh submitted one LSF job per N. Each job reserved a whole node and ran the serial program and the MPI program at P = 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 24 three times, with N and the number of steps fixed (strong scaling), merging off, one thread per rank and ranks bound to cores, socket 0 first.
  • Step loop: simulation_time_s, the time of the time steps on the slowest rank. Whole launch: wall time of each launch, measured by the job script. Start-up: whole launch minus the program's own total_time_s.
  • The numbers are embedded in this file by amdahl/make_page.py from the outputs of amdahl/amdahl_analysis.py. The table of all sweeps covers every job of this code version, everything else the latest sweep of each N.
  • Not covered yet: more than one node (the Allgather over InfiniBand), N between 100 and 1,000 (N = 300 still has to run), N above 5,000, other parallelisations, and the lower clock frequency when all 24 cores are busy, which is not separated from the other losses.
