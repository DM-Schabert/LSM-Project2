Communication schemes: Allgather, ring and 2D, on one node and on two

The same program with three ways of getting the positions to the ranks, all with the same tiled force kernel (--tile-rows 0): Allgather (v1), a ring with and without overlap, and the 2D force decomposition. Strong scaling at fixed N, first on one node (C2), then on two full nodes up to P = 48 (C3), where the ring with and without overlap is C4. How the 2D decomposition works: 2d_decomposition.html.

What the second node brings

T(24) / T(48): how much faster a step gets when the second node joins. 2.0 would double the speed.

How much the network costs

v1: the Allgatherv per step, for the fastest rank (no waiting in it) and averaged over the ranks, and its share of the step. P = 24 is one node, P = 32 and 48 are two.

At P = 32 (24 ranks on one node, 8 on the other) the Allgatherv is several times slower than at P = 48, even for the fastest rank. Most likely Open MPI picks a different algorithm for 32 ranks; not checked. It costs little per step.

Overlap on and off (C4)

The ring with overlap (Isend/Irecv while computing) against the blocking ring (Sendrecv), time per step. Below 1 the overlap helps.

Where the time of a step goes

Campaign and N as above. Forces: the slowest rank's force kernel. Communication and waiting (the stop Allreduce): averaged over the ranks. For the ring and 2D the communication also contains waiting, because their exchanges lie between the parts of the force computation.

Nodes

The serial program is the same on every node, so its step time shows how fast a node is: the median over N of its time against the median of all nodes. All six nodes have the same CPU; n-62-21-75 is still about 12 % faster. Every time on this page is divided by the factor of the slowest node of its run, because a run goes at the pace of its slowest rank. Repeating C2 on other nodes in C3 gave the same times within 1–2 % after this correction (at most 8 %).

How it is measured
  • One Amdahl sweep per scheme and N (amdahl/run_all.sh): the serial run and every P in one job, 3 repeats, every second one in reverse order; the median per P. Labels: v1-tile, v2-ring-tile, v2-ringblk-tile, v3-2d-tile, and the same with -2n for two nodes.
  • Two nodes: NODES=2, --map-by core --bind-to core, so P ≤ 24 stays on the first node and P = 32 has 24 + 8 ranks.
  • Steps per run: N = 50: 20 000 (C2) or 5 000 (C3); N = 1 000: 300; N = 5 000: 30; N = 20 000: 3. Merging off. Time per step: simulation_time_s / iterations of the slowest rank.
  • Every run of C2 and C3 passed C1 before (results/validate_30082644.csv); the 2D copies of each position agreed exactly in every run.
