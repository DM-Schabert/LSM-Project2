2D force decomposition

The table shows all forces of one time step: row i holds the forces on star i, column j the forces that star j exerts. Every cell is one pair of stars. The only question is which rank computes which cells, and which positions it needs to know for that.

Click a rank (in the table or in the grid), then step through. “v1” and “Ring” show the same table in the other decompositions. At the bottom: what the measurements on the cluster showed.

Where the time of a step goes

    

      
Ranks P

    

    

    

    
Unit: multiples of the ideal step time T(1)/P. At 1.0 (dashed) the scaling would be perfect; everything to the right of it is lost time. The parts are averaged over the ranks; the total length is the slowest rank. “Forces” above 1 means that all ranks together take longer for the same pairs than one rank alone. “Communication” includes waiting for the neighbour in the ring and waiting in the row Allreduce in 2D; “Waiting” is the stop Allreduce at the end of the step, where the rest of the load imbalance ends up.
